# Pip's Big Day in Whispering Hollow

**Runtime target: 6:00 | Style anchor:** *Warm Pixar-style 3D animated forest, soft painterly golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, cinematic handheld-but-smooth camera moves, high-detail fur/feather textures, family-friendly, whimsical firefly lighting accents.* (Append this anchor to every clip prompt below for visual consistency.)

**Cast:** Pip (hedgehog, turning 6, birthday pal) · Finn (fox, adventurous leader) · Remy (squirrel, snack-obsessed and quick) · Blu (bluebird, musical and cheerful) · Prof (owl, wise and fussy) · Luna (wolf pup, gentle and dreamy) · Biscuit (golden retriever pup, goofy and food-driven) · Jane (narrator, warm storybook voice)

---

## 1. FULL PRODUCTION SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — Forest Morning (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** Golden sunrise through the canopy, dew on ferns, camera glides low through undergrowth toward a hollow oak.
**Jane (narrator, V.O.):** "In the heart of Whispering Hollow, one very special morning was beginning..."

### SCENE 2 — Finn's Wake-Up Call (0:15–0:35)
**Visual:** Finn bursts from his den clutching a rolled leaf-scroll invitation, tail flagging with excitement.
**Finn:** "Today's the day! Pip's turning six — and we've got a party to build!"

### SCENE 3 — Gathering the Crew (0:35–1:00)
**Visual:** Finn dashes tree to tree — raps on Remy's hollow, whistles up to Blu's nest, taps Prof's window, nudges Luna awake under a fern frond, splashes creek water on sleeping Biscuit.
**Remy:** "A party? With snacks?! I'm in!"
**Blu:** *(yawns, then perks up)* "Ooh — I'll bring the music!"
**Prof:** "Punctuality, as always, will be key."
**Luna:** *(soft, sleepy smile)* "Pip's birthday already? Time flies."
**Biscuit:** *(sputtering water)* "Wha— party?! Did somebody say CAKE?!"

### SCENE 4 — Decorating the Clearing (1:00–1:30)
**Visual:** The crew strings firefly-jar lights, hangs leaf bunting, arranges mushroom-cap stools around a moss table.
**Remy:** "Careful with the acorn lanterns, Biscuit!"
**Biscuit:** "No promises!"
**Prof:** *(measuring bunting with a twig ruler)* "Symmetry, everyone. Symmetry."

### SCENE 5 — Pip's Entrance (1:30–1:50)
**Visual:** Pip peeks through the bushes, spots the glowing, decorated clearing, gasps.
**All (together):** "SURPRISE!!"
**Pip:** "You guys... this is the best morning ever!"

### SCENE 6 — Gift-Passing Circle (1:50–2:50)
*(Everyone seated in a circle around Pip; each gift beat runs ~10 seconds.)*

**Finn** *(hands over a carved wooden compass):* "So you never lose your way exploring — but you always find your way back to us."
**Pip:** "Finn, it's perfect."

**Remy** *(presents an acorn-shell box of roasted seeds):* "Snacks for the road. Don't eat 'em all in one day — like I would."
**Pip:** *(laughing)* "No promises!"

**Blu** *(flutters down with a feather-and-twig wind chime):* "Made it myself. Catches the wind and hums your favorite tune."
**Pip:** "It's beautiful, Blu."

**Prof** *(offers a small leather-bound book of forest maps):* "Every good explorer needs a map, my boy. Or, in your case, six of them."
**Pip:** "I'll fill it with new places!"

**Luna** *(gives a soft moss-woven blanket):* "For stargazing nights. So you're never cold looking up."
**Pip:** "I love it, Luna. Thank you."

**Biscuit** *(proudly holds up a slightly squished berry pie):* "I made you a pie! I— may have tested a corner. Or three."
**Pip:** *(laughing)* "Biscuit! That's so exactly you."

### SCENE 7 — Party Games (2:50–4:20)

**Game 1 — Acorn Toss (2:50–3:20)**
**Visual:** Line of mushroom stumps; acorns lobbed into a hollow log.
**Remy:** "Bullseye! Told you I had good aim."
**Finn:** "Beginner's luck!"

**Game 2 — Forest Hide-and-Seek (3:20–3:50)**
**Visual:** Pip counts at the oak trunk; others dart behind ferns and mushrooms. Biscuit hides badly, tail sticking out from a bush.
**Pip:** "Found you, Biscuit — your tail gave it away!"
**Biscuit:** "It always does."

**Game 3 — Bramble Obstacle Race (3:50–4:20)**
**Visual:** Everyone hops over roots, ducks under a fallen log, hops stepping stones. Luna wins gracefully; Prof "judges" with a leaf-blade stopwatch.
**Prof:** "And Luna takes the crown — by a whisker!"
**Luna:** *(shy smile)* "Just quick paws, that's all."

### SCENE 8 — The Cake Moment (4:20–5:20)
**Visual:** Blu and Remy roll out a berry-and-honey cake on a broad leaf platter, topped with six gently glowing firefly "candles."
**All (singing):**
*"Happy, happy birthday, dear Pip, today, / Whispering Hollow sings this way, / Six years of you, bright as the sun, / Here's to all the fun to come!"*
**Visual:** Pip closes his eyes, makes a wish; the fireflies lift off the cake and twinkle upward instead of "blowing out."
**Pip:** "Best birthday wish ever."
**Finn:** "What'd you wish for?"
**Pip:** "If I tell you, it won't come true!"
*(Everyone laughs)*

### SCENE 9 — Sunset Goodbye (5:20–6:00)
**Visual:** Golden hour, the group huddled close, fireflies drifting around them in a slow spiral, group hug, wide pull-back to show the whole glowing clearing under early stars.
**Pip:** "Thank you all. This was the best day in the whole forest."
**Luna:** "Every year, Pip. Every single year."
**Biscuit:** "Same time next year — but bigger cake!"
*(Group laughter)*
**Jane (narrator, V.O.):** "And so, under a blanket of stars, Whispering Hollow tucked in another perfect birthday... until next year."
